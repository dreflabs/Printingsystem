# Warehouse Dashboard

Large actions:
- RECEIVE MATERIAL
- ISSUE MATERIAL
- STORE JOB
- FIND JOB
- RELEASE JOB
- SCAN QR

## Store Job
Scan Job QR -> Scan Location QR -> Confirm quantity -> Store

## Find Job
Scan Job QR -> show current location -> show status

## Release
Scan Job QR -> verify payment -> verify receiver -> Release

Warehouse must never need to manually search through piles of paper to find finished goods.
